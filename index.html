    Health & Vaccinations
    RecommendedHepatitis A, Typhoid, routine (MMR, Tdap, flu)
    MalariaVery low risk in tourist areas. No prophylaxis needed for this itinerary.
    WaterDrink bottled or filtered only — even in good hotels. Brush teeth with bottled.
    Food safetyCooked street food is generally safe. Avoid raw salads at street stalls.
    PackImodium, oral rehydration salts, blister plasters, sunscreen SPF 50+
    Altitude sicknessDiamox — ask your doctor before the trip. Take for Acatenango.
    ⚠ Traveller's diarrhea is common first few days. Bring Imodium. Don't let it ruin day one.

    Weather in December

    Lake Atitlan18–24°C, sunny and clear. December = peak dry season. Best lake conditions of the year.
    GeneralDecember is dry season across Guatemala. Rain is rare. Skies are crystal clear by morning.

    Cultural Etiquette
    PhotographyAlways ask. "¿Puedo tomar una foto?" At Chichi, many vendors will say no — respect it.
    ChurchesCover shoulders and knees. Remove hats. No flash photography inside.
    VillagesDress modestly outside tourist hubs. A small purchase before photographing goes a long way.
    Tipping~10% at restaurants. Q20–50 for guides. Q5–10 for bag help. Not expected at street stalls.
    BargainingExpected at markets (start at 50% of asking price). Never bargain at restaurants or supermarkets.
    DressConservative in villages. Anything goes in Antigua and Atitlan.
    Altitude guide
    Toronto~77m — your baseline
    Guatemala City~1,500m — might feel slightly breathless first day
    Antigua~1,530m — most people feel fine after 24hrs. Drink extra water.
    Chichicastenango~2,071m — noticeable. Short of breath on stairs. Normal.
    Lake Atitlan~1,562m — similar to Antigua. Fine after the first day.
    Acatenango basecamp~3,600m — most people get mild headache + fatigue. Normal. Sleep meds help.
    Acatenango summit~3,976m — exertion at this altitude is serious. Pace extremely slowly. If severely nauseous, descend.
    Altitude sickness symptoms: headache, nausea, dizziness, poor sleep. Mild = rest + water. Severe (confusion, can't walk straight) = descend immediately.

    Apps to download before you go
    InDriver
    Rideshare in Guatemala City. Never take an unmarked street taxi in GC. InDriver only.
    Google Maps
    Download Guatemala offline before departing — works without any signal.
    Maps.me
    Better offline maps than Google for rural Guatemala. Great for Semuc and lake villages.
    WhatsApp
    Tour operators, La Casa del Mundo, and most hotels communicate via WhatsApp.
    XE Currency
    Live CAD → GTQ rates. Useful at markets and ATMs to sanity-check prices.
    iTranslate / Google Translate
    Download Spanish offline. Camera translate mode is useful for menus in smaller villages.
    Luggage tip: Acatenango requires a day pack for the hike (leave main bags at your Antigua hotel or hotel storage). The lake is accessible by boat only — no need to haul everything to every village.
